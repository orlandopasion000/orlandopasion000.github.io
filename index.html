I am Sir Orlando V. Pasion, Jr., Teacher VI at Nancalobasaan National High School under DepEd Region I — Ilocos Region. Mathematics is the discipline through which I teach problem-solving, confidence, and lifelong learning.

Currently pursuing my Doctor of Education in Educational Management at Urdaneta City University, I am preparing for school leadership while strengthening my practice across the five PPSSH domains.

I believe in the fusion of tradition and technology — where chalk meets code, and every student discovers that mathematics is a bridge to possibility.
