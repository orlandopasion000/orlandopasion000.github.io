Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026 · Version 1.0

Daily Recharge Alarm ("the Application", "we", "us") is designed with a fundamental commitment to privacy. The Application is a mindful schedule rhythm, rest timer, and alarm tool built to help you recharge and manage your day peacefully.

1. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, IP address, or location.
  • We do not use advertising networks, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics (such as Google Analytics or Facebook SDK).
  • We do not operate remote user account databases; no account registration or login is ever required.

2. 100% On-Device Local Storage

All of your application data—including your custom daily alarms, active days of the week, audio volume levels, selected clock themes, and soundscape preferences—is stored strictly on your own device using standard web browser storage APIs (localStorage and Cache Storage).

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export a backup JSON file or copy a shareable link yourself.

3. Device Permissions & System Usage

The Application may request the following device permissions solely to provide its core alarm and rest functionality:

  • Audio & Media: Used solely to play offline relaxing nature soundscapes (green noise, rain, waves, river, piano) and your selected alarm music clips.
  • Notifications: Used solely to alert you when a scheduled alarm or rest session arrives. Notification data is generated locally on your device and is not sent to any cloud push server.
  • Screen Wake Lock (Optional): Used only during active Rest Mode or when alarms are armed to prevent your display from prematurely sleeping if you wish to keep the clock visible.

4. Data Backup & Deletion

Because all data resides exclusively in your device's local storage, you have total control over it at all times:

  • You can back up your schedule at any time via the Export Backup (.json) feature.
  • You can permanently erase all data at any time by clearing your web browser's storage/cache or clicking Reset Schedule within the app.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not collect data from any user, including children under the age of 13. It is fully compliant with COPPA, GDPR, and family policies across mobile app stores.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the development team directly through our public GitHub repository at github.com/orlandopasion000/orlandopasion000.github.io.
